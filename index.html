०२ · About Us

Stories from the ghats of thought

Tatraapi Films is a new production house based in Mysuru. We tell stories rooted in Indian thought for an audience anywhere on earth — unhurried, carefully made, and unafraid of silence.

Mission

To make films that think

We make cinema that treats the audience as a seeker, not a consumer. Each film starts from a question the Indian philosophical tradition has carried for centuries, and follows it honestly through character, image and sound.

We work from Mysuru, with collaborators who care about craft as much as meaning.

Vision

Indian thought, in a global language

We want a viewer in any country to walk out of a Tatraapi film carrying an idea they did not walk in with — and to feel it was told to them plainly, without translation notes.

Old wisdom and new tools belong at the same table. We intend to seat them there.

Four things we keep returning to

कर्म

Karma

Every act leaves a trace. Our stories follow the trace — how a choice travels through a life, and through the lives around it.
